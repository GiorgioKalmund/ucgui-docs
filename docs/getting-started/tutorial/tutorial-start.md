In this little mini series of individual tutorials we will explore the foundation of UCGUI and its components. 
Through the use of code examples and some accompanying text we will not only take a look at how UCGUI is structured, but also try to explain some of the decisions behind the resulting API.


If you have already used UCGUI and maybe just need a bit of a refresher you might want 
to take a look at the [quickstart page](../quickstart.md) instead for a quick refresher.
If you are new here, welcome! We hope UCGUI can provide at least a bit of value for
you or your team :).

This tutorial aims to cover most of the basics, so you can get straight into it afterwards.
If you want even more information about a specific component then feel free to 
simply check out its deidicated page under ['Components'](../../category/components)!

:::
